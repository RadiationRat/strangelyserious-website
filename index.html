Boss Tracker

A Windows app for Wizard101 players. It reads the game screen (no memory reading or injection), spots the current quest or boss, and shows an on-screen warning with that boss's tricks and counters. Free for the community.

Barnyard Expanded

A fan expansion for the 2006 PC game Barnyard, built by working with the game's decompiled code and data. Its headline feature is Night Barn Hold'em: a full Texas Hold'em poker minigame with a 3D table, custom card art, betting, and an opponent picker where you choose who from the farm sits down to play.

What an AI search audit includes

We ask ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google's AI Overviews a set of questions about your business, several times each, to measure how often you're mentioned. We check that AI tools can read your site and give you a fix for every issue we find. About 6 weeks after the fixes go in, we measure again. If your numbers haven't improved, the next re-check is free.

We can't promise more mentions or traffic. AI tools pick their own sources, and their answers change often.
